1. Zero-Upload Local Processing

Digital Asset Forge computes all 300 DPI calculations, artwork canvas crops, ZIP bundle packaging, and Etsy SEO outputs strictly inside your browser or on your local loopback daemon (127.0.0.1:8118). Your artwork files are never uploaded to external servers or used for AI training.

Only your Light/Dark theme preference (daf_theme) is stored locally in localStorage. No analytics scripts, external font CDNs, tracking cookies, or advertising networks are loaded.
