Frequently Asked Questions

Is Digital Asset Forge free to use?

Yes. Digital Asset Forge is 100% free and open access with zero paywalls—all pack generation, PNG framing, ZIP exports, and MCP tools are unlocked by default. Optional community support is available via Buy Us a Coffee (Stripe) or Solana Tip (GG2eQbVh8ut1BK6j1A2tz7pSySdLpD38BgyEhGcxdXoK).

Why does Etsy enforce a 20 MB upload cap?

Etsy restricts each digital attachment to 20 MB and up to 5 files per listing. Digital Asset Forge greedily bin-packs your 5 master 300 DPI files and buyer printing guide under an 18.0 MB target per archive (2 ZIPs, 36.1 MB combined).

Can I use these packs on Shopify, Gumroad, or WooCommerce?

Yes. Every ratio specification, buyer printing guide, and ZIP bundle is platform-agnostic and works directly with Shopify Digital Downloads, Gumroad, WooCommerce, and Patreon.
