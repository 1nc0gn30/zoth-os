    5-Stage Deterministic Research Architecture

    Pure Python 3.9+ Stdlib
    1. 01
      Tree-of-Thought Query Planner (planner.py)

      Decomposes any research prompt into domain-specific angles (3 / 5 / 8 depths) and generates exact-match, Boolean, and adversarial sub-queries calibrated to Q_target > 2.0 breadth coverage.

    2. 02
      SSRF-Guarded Document Fetch & HTML Extractor (crawler.py + url_guard.py)

      Enforces http/https schemes only, blocks loopback, RFC1918, CGNAT, and link-local IMDS targets (including redirect hops), strips boilerplate HTML, and scores domain credibility α_cred ∈ [0, 100].

    3. 03
      Directed Evidence Provenance Graph (evidence_graph.py)

      Constructs directed source-to-claim edges, executes DFS backtracking to catch circular reporting or echo chambers, and computes TrustRank eigenvector authority scores.

    4. 04
      Cross-Source Contradiction & Stance Matrix (contradiction_detector.py)

      Audits extracted factual claims for >20% numeric divergence, temporal mismatches, and sentiment polarity conflicts to separate corroborated consensus from disputed claims.

    5. 05
      Grounded Synthesis & Multi-Format Export (synthesizer.py + report_engine.py)

      Produces inline-cited Markdown, XSS-escaped HTML, structured JSON, BibTeX (@article / @misc), and ZIP bundles with zero external runtime dependencies.
