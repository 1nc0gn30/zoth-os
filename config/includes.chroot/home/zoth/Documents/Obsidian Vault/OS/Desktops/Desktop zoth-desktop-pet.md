# ZothOS Desktop Pet HUD

Interactive Celestial Griffin, Dragon, Phoenix & All-Seeing Eye Companion.

## Desktop Entry Details

- **Name:** `ZothOS Desktop Pet HUD`
- **Generic Name:** `Alchemical AI Companion & Mascot`
- **Comment:** `Interactive Celestial Griffin, Dragon, Phoenix & All-Seeing Eye Companion`
- **Exec:** `/usr/local/bin/zoth-pet-hud`
- **Icon:** `/usr/share/pixmaps/zoth-pet-hud.png`
- **Terminal:** `false`
- **Type:** `Application`
- **Categories:** `Utility;Amusement;ArtificialIntelligence;`
- **Startup Notify:** `true`

- **Actions:** `Eye`, `Aquila`, `Draco`, `Ignis`, `Kitsune`, `Lycan`
- **File:** `/usr/share/applications/zoth-desktop-pet.desktop`

## Launch Configuration & Behavior

The desktop launcher runs the ZothOS Desktop Pet heads-up display via `/usr/local/bin/zoth-pet-hud`. Through desktop context actions, users can directly summon specific companions: All-Seeing Eye (`--pet eye`), Celestial Griffin (`--pet aquila`), Alchemical Dragon (`--pet draco`), Fire Phoenix (`--pet ignis`), Cyber Fox (`--pet kitsune`), or Cyber Wolf (`--pet lycan`).
