# firmware-mediatek

Binary firmware for MediaTek and Ralink chips for networking, SoCs and media. This package contains the binary firmware for MediaTek devices such as ethernet/Wi-Fi/Bluetooth chips, SoCs and other chips and devices made by MediaTek and Ralink.
