# python3-tqdm

fast, extensible progress bar for Python 3 and CLI tool. tqdm (read taqadum, تقدّم) means “progress” in Arabic. tqdm instantly makes your loops show a smart progress meter, just by wrapping any iterable with "tqdm(iterable)".
