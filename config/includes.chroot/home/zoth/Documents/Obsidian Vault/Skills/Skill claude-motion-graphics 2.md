# claude-motion-graphics

`claude-motion-graphics` is an **AGY** motion design skill and prompt blueprint for creating viral product launch films, 15-second showreels, and code-first browser animation assets.

- **Origin:** AGY (`~/.gemini/config/skills/claude-motion-graphics/SKILL.md`)
- **When to load:** Load when producing high-impact product video scripts, 15-second launch showreels, hero section motion graphics, or Canvas/GSAP web animations.

### Core Principles & Architecture
- **Code-First Animation vs Diffusion Video:** Replaces blurry, morphing diffusion video with programmatic choreography (HTML5 Canvas, GSAP, Remotion) featuring crisp vector typography and deterministic rendering.
- **Hero Anchor Philosophy:** Every sequence centers on a continuous geometric anchor shape (e.g., glowing diamond, prism, or laser beam) that transitions seamlessly across scenes to eliminate visual dead space.
- **Snappy Motion Curves:** Enforces high-energy snap easing (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`) and precise 120 BPM beat synchronization.
- **Production Blueprints:**
  - *15-Second Showreel:* Rapid 6-scene teaser cutting on the beat for social launch announcements.
  - *30-Second Launch Film:* Structured narrative covering problem provocation, solution reveal, telemetry blitz, and branded payoff.
  - *In-App Hero Motion:* Embed templates for lightweight interactive canvas loops or video hero modals.
- **Companion Toolkit:** Pairs with [[Skill claude-motion]] for Remotion video rendering and procedural audio generation.
