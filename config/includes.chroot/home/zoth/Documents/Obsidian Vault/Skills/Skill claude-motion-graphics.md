# claude-motion-graphics

`claude-motion-graphics` is a **Grok** motion design skill and prompt blueprint for producing viral 15-second showreels, 30-second product launch videos, and deterministic web motion graphics.

- **Origin:** Grok (`~/.grok/skills/claude-motion-graphics/SKILL.md`)
- **When to load:** Load when creating video showreel prompts, launch film scene breakdowns, or interactive in-browser motion graphics using Canvas or GSAP.

### Core Principles & Architecture
- **Code-Directed Choreography:** Uses programmatic animation (Canvas, GSAP, SVG, Remotion) rather than generative AI video models, ensuring sharp typography, stable geometries, and deterministic camera paths.
- **Hero Anchor Technique:** Directs visual attention using a continuous signature shape or beam that morphs across scenes without cutting to dead space.
- **High-Tension Timing:** Utilizes aggressive snap easing curves (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`) and precise tempo synchronization matched to 120 BPM track beats.
- **Video Archetypes & Prompts:**
  - *15s Creative Director Showreel:* 6 fast-paced scenes designed for high-conversion social announcements.
  - *30s Product Launch Showcase:* Structured narrative moving from problem setup to UI feature demonstration and call to action.
  - *Interactive In-Browser Loops:* Canvas-based kinetic particle backgrounds and video hero modals.
- **Companion Toolkit:** Complements motion engineering tools like [[Skill claude-motion]] for automated rendering and sound design.
