# weekly-review-planning

> **Workstation Skill (Hermes)** — Origin: Hermes skill (workstation skill, not a Zoth product).

A structured productivity workflow for executing recurring weekly resets, reconciling past commitments, and planning the upcoming week across calendar, task, and note systems.

### 7-Step Review Procedure
1. **Window & Scope:** Establishes time boundaries, primary task/calendar stores, and enforces draft-only proposals before applying mutations.
2. **Calendar Audit:** Inspects completed events for missed follow-ups and scans the 1–2 week horizon for capacity constraints and deadlines.
3. **Inbox Triage:** Surfaces items from notes (`obsidian`, `notion`) and flagged threads (`email-inbox-triage`) into actionable states.
4. **Project Reconciliation:** Evaluates active initiatives for next actions, blockers, and stale progress.
5. **Commitments & Waiting:** Tracks promises made to or owed by collaborators without assuming silence indicates completion.
6. **Capacity-Aware Planning:** Prioritizes high-consequence outcomes that realistically fit within calendar availability.
7. **Approved Writes:** Applies changes to external tools only upon operator confirmation, verifying written records back from providers.

### When to Load
- Running scheduled weekly reviews or reset workflows (often scheduled via cron).
- Auditing slipped deliverables, stale projects, and outstanding follow-ups.
