# FaqsPage

`FaqsPage` is the interactive architectural FAQ and knowledge oracle in Zoth Studio, rendered at route `[[Route faqs|/faqs]]`.

## Rendered Capabilities

- **Interactive Oracle Query Terminal**: In-browser query engine matching operator inquiries against the studio's architectural specifications, reporting computed confidence ratings and formatted specifications.
- **Quick Prompt Exploration**: Provides 10 curated exploration chips (covering Zero-Egress Invariant, WebGPU Acceleration, Zoth CLI Workflow, STDP Synaptic Decay, and 3-Agent Byzantine Consensus).
- **Structured FAQ Accordion Matrix**: 17 in-depth architectural entries grouped across 6 categories:
  - *Architecture & Security*: Zero-egress network boundaries, air-gapped guarantees, and Argon2id hardware vaults.
  - *Memory & AI*: Biomorphic STDP synaptic plasticity models and local Ollama model bridges.
  - *Consensus & Tools*: Byzantine fault-tolerant AST resolution, WebGPU compute, and client-side AST transformations.
  - *CLI & Deployment*: Local hypervisor orchestration and Netlify edge prerendering.
- **Search & Direct Navigation**: Instant text filtering across questions, answers, and tags, with automatic scroll-to-accordion deep links.

- **Source File:** `zoth-studio-v2/src/pages/FaqsPage.jsx`
- **Parent / Route:** Served at `/faqs`
