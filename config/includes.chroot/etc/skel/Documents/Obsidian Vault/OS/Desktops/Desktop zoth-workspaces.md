# Restore 8 virtual desktops

Put the Samsung on its own screen and keep 8 named virtual desktops

- **Exec:** `/usr/local/bin/zoth-konsole --pause --title "Zoth Workspaces" -- zoth-workspaces desktops`
- **Categories:** Settings;System;
- **File:** `/usr/share/applications/zoth-workspaces.desktop`
