# Undercover

Disguise Mode, Identity Mask & Win11 Undercover Theme

## Desktop Entry Details

- **Name:** `Undercover`
- **Comment:** `Disguise Mode, Identity Mask & Win11 Undercover Theme`
- **Exec:** `/usr/local/bin/zoth-undercover`
- **Icon:** `zoth-undercover`
- **Terminal:** `false`
- **Type:** `Application`
- **Categories:** `Settings;System;Security;`
- **Keywords:** `undercover;disguise;mask;identity;win11;theme;stealth;`
- **MIME Type:** `application/x-zoth-undercover;`
- **Startup Notify:** `true`
- **File:** `/usr/share/applications/zoth-undercover.desktop`

## Launch Configuration & Behavior

The desktop entry integrates disguise mode and identity masking into the desktop environment under Settings, System, and Security. Launching the entry executes `/usr/local/bin/zoth-undercover` directly without opening a separate terminal, toggling stealth and appearance profiles while dispatching startup notifications to the window manager.
