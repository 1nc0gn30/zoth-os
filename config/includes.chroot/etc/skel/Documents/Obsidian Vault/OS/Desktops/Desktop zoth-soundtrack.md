# Zoth Hermetic Audio Studio

Satie, Chopin, Debussy & Bach Hermetic Focus Studio.

## Desktop Entry Details

- **Name:** `Zoth Hermetic Audio Studio`
- **Comment:** `Satie, Chopin, Debussy & Bach Hermetic Focus Studio`
- **Exec:** `zoth-music`
- **Icon:** `zoth-soundtrack`
- **Terminal:** `false`
- **Type:** `Application`
- **Categories:** `AudioVideo;Audio;Player;`
- **Keywords:** `audio;music;classical;piano;satie;chopin;bach;focus;`
- **Startup Notify:** `false`
- **File:** `/usr/share/applications/zoth-soundtrack.desktop`

## Launch Configuration & Behavior

Registers Zoth Hermetic Audio Studio under `AudioVideo`, `Audio`, and `Player` desktop categories. Activating the launcher executes `zoth-music` directly (`Terminal=false`) to play classical focus audio compositions (including Satie, Chopin, Debussy, and Bach). Startup notification is disabled (`StartupNotify=false`), and it references the `zoth-soundtrack` icon asset.
