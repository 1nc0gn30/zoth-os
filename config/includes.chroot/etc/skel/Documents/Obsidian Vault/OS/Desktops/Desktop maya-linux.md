# Zoth Maya Video Studio

Wrap Screen Recording, Motion Timeline & Video Production.

## Desktop Entry Details

- **Name:** `Zoth Maya Video Studio`
- **Generic Name:** `Device Mockup & Video Studio`
- **Comment:** `Wrap Screen Recording, Motion Timeline & Video Production`
- **Exec:** `maya`
- **Icon:** `/usr/share/pixmaps/maya-linux.png`
- **Terminal:** `false`
- **Type:** `Application`
- **Categories:** `AudioVideo;Video;AudioVideoEditing;`
- **Startup Notify:** `true`
- **File:** `/usr/share/applications/maya-linux.desktop`

## Launch Configuration & Behavior

The desktop entry registers the Maya video and mockup production suite within multimedia menus under AudioVideo, Video, and AudioVideoEditing. When invoked, it directly executes the `maya` binary without an external terminal wrapper, displaying the dedicated `maya-linux.png` pixmap icon and providing desktop startup notifications.
