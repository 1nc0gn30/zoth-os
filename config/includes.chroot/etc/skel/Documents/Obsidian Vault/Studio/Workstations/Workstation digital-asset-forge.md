# Digital Asset Forge // 300 DPI Wall Art Pack Generator

High-resolution 300 DPI multi-ratio art pack generator, 20MB Etsy ZIP split optimizer, and automated customer printing guide synthesizer.

## Specification & Taxonomy

- **ID:** `digital-asset-forge`
- **Name:** Digital Asset Forge // 300 DPI Wall Art Pack Generator
- **Type:** `integrated_tool` (Standalone sovereign micro-tool and CLI utility in the Tool Arsenal)
- **Band:** Build
- **Static Path:** `/studio/digital-asset-forge.html`
- **In-App Route:** `/tools/digital-asset-forge`
- **Repository ID:** `digital-asset-forge`
- **Launch Command:** `./bin/digital-asset-forge server --port 8118`

## Operational Profile

Registered in the Zoth Studio v2 matrix under the **Build** functional band as an integrated sovereign tool. Designed as a standalone utility for synthesizing multi-ratio art packs at 300 DPI, optimizing digital files within Etsy's 20MB ZIP limit, and generating customer printing guides. Operates via static interface, the `/tools/digital-asset-forge` route, or its local CLI service.
