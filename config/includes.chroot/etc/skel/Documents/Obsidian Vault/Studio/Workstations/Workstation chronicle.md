# Zoth Chronicle & Engineering Horizon Roadmap

The **Zoth Chronicle & Engineering Horizon Roadmap** is registered in the Zoth Studio v2 workstations directory (`src/data/workstations.js`) under Studio Cockpits & Developer IDEs.

### Core Functionality
Milestone timeline, system architecture chronicle, and release telemetry logs.

### Workstation Metadata
- **ID:** `chronicle`
- **Name:** Zoth Chronicle & Engineering Horizon Roadmap
- **Type:** `cockpit` (multi-pane developer IDE and visual operational cockpit for human-agent engineering)
- **Functional Band:** Observe
- **Static Entrypoint:** `/studio/chronicle.html`
- **In-App Route:** `/workstations/chronicle`

### Operational Role
Classified under the Observe band to provide developmental visibility across the platform, tracking milestone timelines, maintaining the system architecture record, and displaying telemetry from historical release phases.
