# AdytumShowcasePage

`AdytumShowcasePage` is the overview showcase page for the Adytum subsystem in Zoth Studio, rendered at route `[[Route adytum|/adytum]]`.

## Rendered Capabilities

- **Hero & Positioning**: Highlights automated AI workflow orchestration with contract validation and zero-egress execution.
- **Core Architecture Pillars**:
  - *Chained Workflows*: Connects AI calls into automated, multi-step reasoning pipelines.
  - *Schema-Validated I/O*: Validates inputs and outputs against strict schemas at each boundary.
  - *Plan & Verify*: Incorporates explicit planning and verification phases at every execution stage.
  - *Runs Locally*: Guarantees all model interactions and data remain confined to local loopback.
- **Target Audience**: Focuses on AI engineers building production pipelines, teams automating complex LLM procedures, and builders requiring deterministic reproducibility.
- **Action CTAs**: Links directly to the deep interactive workbench at `[[Route adytum-docs|/adytum/docs]]` and the Adytum Alchemist repository.

- **Source File:** `zoth-studio-v2/src/pages/AdytumShowcasePage.jsx`
- **Parent / Route:** Served at `/adytum` (deep documentation and workbench at `[[Route adytum-docs|/adytum/docs]]`)
