# songwriting-and-ai-music

Workstation skill from Hermes (not a Zoth product). Authored by Teknium (teknium1) and Hermes Agent.

- **Origin:** Hermes skill
- **Path:** `~/.hermes/hermes-agent/skills/creative/songwriting-and-ai-music/SKILL.md`

## Overview

Guides lyrical composition, song structuring, parody adaptation, and prompt engineering for AI music generators such as Suno AI and local engines (`heartmula`, `audiocraft`).

## When to Load

- Composing song lyrics, verses, hooks, pre-choruses, or bridges.
- Crafting Suno style descriptions (genre, instruments, vocal persona, dynamic trajectory).
- Adapting existing songs or writing parodies (syllable counts, stress patterns, rhyme mapping).
- Adding performance metatags (`[Verse]`, `[Chorus]`, `[Whispered]`, `[Belted]`, `[Guitar Solo]`).
- Formatting lyrics with phonetic respellings for AI vocal synthesis.

## Core Techniques

- **Structure & Dynamics:** Uses classic song skeletons (ABABCB, AABA) mapped along dynamic energy curves ("whisper to roar to whisper").
- **Prosody & Rhyme:** Balances perfect, family, assonance, and slant rhymes with internal echoes.
- **Suno Prompt Engineering:** Combines genre, mood, era, instrumentation, and vocal persona in the Style field, avoiding trademarked artist names.
- **Phonetic Tuning:** Employs hyphenation ("re-search"), phonetic respelling ("thru"), and casing/punctuation to dictate AI vocal timing and intensity.
